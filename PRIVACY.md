# Privacy

Kids Learn collects nothing.

- **No network access.** The app does not declare the `INTERNET` permission, so Android
  will not let it open a connection. It makes no network calls to anyone.
- **No accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking** of any kind, and no
  third-party SDKs that could do these.
- **What is stored on the phone:** two settings (speech speed, and whether cards are read
  out automatically), in the app's private storage. Android's own backup may copy them to
  your Google account or a new phone if you have backup turned on; the app itself sends
  them nowhere. Quiz stars are not stored at all.
- **Speech:** words are handed to the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone (for
  example Google's Speech Services) to be spoken. That engine is a separate app with its
  own privacy policy. With the voice installed it works offline. When you tap "Install
  voice", the app only opens the engine's or the system's voice settings; any download
  happens there, not in Kids Learn.
- **Permissions:** none.
